# Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Ingredients
- 200g unsalted butter, soft
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups + 4 tbsp of self raising flour (about 155g)
- 1/2 tsp baking soda 
- 1 cup of brown sugar
- 1 cuf of chocolate chip cookies, 70% cocoa

## Instructions
1. Line a parchement paper on a baking sheet
2. Mix all the ingredients
3. Form small ping-pong sized balls from the dough
4. Put the dough balls on the baking sheet leaving space between them
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 170°C for 15-18 minutes, until they are golden
